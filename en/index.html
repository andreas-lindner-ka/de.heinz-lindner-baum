The Story

A life dedicated to engineering – a tree built for eternity

Heinz Lindner was born in 1934 and devoted his life to mechanical engineering. As an engineer he understood better than most that true quality takes time – and that the best constructions are those that outlast generations.

Rheinhafen Steam Power Plant, Karlsruhe

Heinz Lindner worked at the Rheinhafen Steam Power Plant – one of the most significant industrial sites in Karlsruhe. As a mechanical engineer he kept the turbines running, maintained the boilers under pressure, and ensured the city received a reliable supply of electricity. Every light that shone in Karlsruhe carried a little of his work.

"A good engineer doesn't think in years – he thinks in decades."

In 1990, a Giant Sequoia was planted in his garden in Karlsruhe – the largest tree species on Earth. While Heinz Lindner spent decades keeping machines running and powering the city, this tree grew quietly alongside him. Today, following his death in 2023, it bears his name: the Heinz Lindner Tree.

The power plant delivered energy to the city. The tree does the same in its own way – through clean air, cooling and life. A more fitting transition from one life's work to the next is hard to imagine.

Urban Climate & Nature

What this tree does for Karlsruhe every single day

Cities like Karlsruhe are getting warmer due to climate change. Trees are not decoration – they are vital infrastructure. A mature Giant Sequoia does daily what engineers would otherwise need machines to accomplish.

The Heinz Lindner Tree is not a private memorial. It works every day for the neighbourhood, for the urban climate, for nature – quietly and without pause. Just like the man it is named after.

Sequoiadendron giganteum

Why a Giant Sequoia?

The Giant Sequoia is no ordinary garden tree. It is the largest living thing nature has ever produced – and one of the longest-lived. A choice that speaks for itself.

Record
Largest tree in the world by volume
Longevity
Up to 3,500 years old
Resilience
Fire-resistant thanks to thick bark
Origin
Sierra Nevada – and now Karlsruhe

The most famous example of the species, General Sherman in Sequoia National Park, California, stands around 84 metres tall, weighs an estimated 1,385 tonnes, and is over 2,000 years old. It is considered the largest living organism on Earth by timber volume.

The Heinz Lindner Tree in Karlsruhe shares its species with this record holder. With a little patience – and a few centuries – who knows what may yet be possible.
